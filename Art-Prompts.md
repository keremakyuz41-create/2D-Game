# Görsel üretim kayıtları

Bu aşamadaki görseller yerleşik image_gen aracıyla üretildi. Sonuçlar oyuna ayrı arka plan ve alfa kanallı karakter sprite'ları olarak eklendi. Özgün kaynak dosyaları yeniden boyutlandırılmadı veya düzenlenmedi.

## street

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual Unity 2D level background, NOT a mockup. Create a landscape image aspect ratio about 3:2, fully filled rectangular playable map in crisp detailed pixel art for a small top-down Chernobyl zombie game. Orthographic overhead view with small visible front faces of buildings as in classic 2D RPG games, NOT isometric. Empty abandoned gray-green cracked asphalt street runs horizontally across the middle 60 percent of the image, wide open traversable fighting space, NO lane divider verticals. Faded yellow dashed center line along the horizontal middle. Upper 20 percent: gray concrete paving sidewalk and crumbling Soviet apartment frontage at the very top border, dark windows, peeling concrete, sparse ivy, two warm amber doorway lamps. Lower 20 percent: concrete sidewalk with gutters, weeds in cracks, and sparse edge fence. A rusted abandoned sedan is parked along the UPPER LEFT roadside, clear of the middle street. Sparse barrels, tiny rubble and weeds ONLY beside pavement edges. Central 60 percent must remain unobstructed walkable asphalt with no large obstacles, cars or barriers. Lighting muted dusk but clearly visible, warm tiny light pools near lamps, palette charcoal, olive, rusty ochre. Detailed chunky intentional pixel clusters, about 768x512 logical pixels, readable game tiles visual. No characters, no zombies, no robot, NO UI, no text, no letters, no labels, no crosshair, no camera perspective, no dramatic fog, no black vignette. The image is a game environment texture which will be placed behind real moving characters, not a screenshot or poster.

## survivor

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: SINGLE game character sprite, transparent background. One full-body male survivor for top-down 2D Chernobyl game, standing and facing RIGHT with visible side/front three-quarter view in classic overhead 2D RPG perspective. Olive military jacket, small olive backpack, brown short hair, dark trousers, black boots, arms held slightly forward to hold a small pistol supplied separately by game engine. No gun in this image. Clear head torso arms two legs silhouette. Crisp chunky pixel art of about 48x64 logical pixels enlarged with sharp nearest-neighbor blocks, dark subtle outline, muted colors, worn clothing, warm skin. Fit complete character centered on canvas with approximately 10 percent transparent margin. Isolated sprite only. Actual transparent alpha, no background, no checkerboard, no ground, no shadow oval, no frame, no sheet, no multiple poses, no labels, no lettering, no UI. A practical modest student Unity game sprite, not detailed portrait concept art.
