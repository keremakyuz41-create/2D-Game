Yerleşik image_gen aracıyla üretildi. Kutu ve tüfek gerçek alfa kanalıyla, depo zemini opak arka plan olarak oluşturuldu. Kaynak pikseller değiştirilmedi; Unity içe aktarma boyut ve sprite pivotunu belirler.

## crate

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single actual Unity 2D destructible supply CRATE sprite. One closed battered wooden military supply box with metal corners, worn olive canvas band, front-facing with slightly visible top face as in top-down 2D pixel adventure games. Crisp chunky pixel art, muted olive-brown wood, dark outlines, tiny scratches, readable rectangular silhouette. About 64x48 logical pixels, sharp edges. Fit entire crate with small transparent margin. No text, no labels, no weapons sticking out, no floor, no cast shadow, no background or checkerboard. Genuine transparent alpha. Match a Chernobyl zombie survival pixel game.

For top-down Chernobyl zombie game.

## depot

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual background map texture for Unity 2D game, not a screenshot or poster. Landscape 3:2 orthographic top-down classic 2D pixel art abandoned supply depot street near fictional Chernobyl. Wide open cracked asphalt street crosses horizontally in central 60 percent, faded pale yellow dashed line along horizontal middle. UPPER 20 percent: cracked concrete sidewalk and decayed blue gray warehouse facade along very top edge, rusty closed roller doors, one dim amber lamp, a couple of dark green barrels at far upper-left edge. LOWER 20 percent: cracked sidewalk, sparse weeds and damaged industrial wire fence along bottom edge. All middle road entirely open and walkable; NO large obstacles, NO crate in central road, NO characters, NO zombies, NO weapons, NO UI or HUD, NO readable text or numbers. Fine detailed crisp pixel clusters, muted blue gray and moss green with rusty brown, readable dusk atmosphere. Small shadows only near edges. Same practical classic pixel survival game style as a ruined street game. Camera overhead, slight visible front faces on warehouse like 2D RPG, not isometric, not 3D.
