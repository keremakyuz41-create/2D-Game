# Yardımcı robot ve santral kapısı görselleri

Yerleşik image_gen aracı kullanıldı. Kaynak PNG'ler `Assets/Art/Companion/` altında gerçek alfa kanallarıyla saklanır. Piksel verileri sonradan değiştirilmedi; Unity içe aktarma sırasında sprite ölçeği ayarlanır.

## robot

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single ACTUAL 2D Unity game sprite on transparent background. Small friendly maintenance companion robot for Chernobyl pixel-art survival adventure: squat square olive gray metal body, two short black tank tracks, one bright cyan rectangular eye screen, small thin antenna and worn metal panels. Facing right in slight overhead 2D RPG view, not isometric, not photoreal. Practical chunky 48x40 logical pixel art with crisp dark outline, fully visible entire robot centered with small transparent margins. No floor, no cast shadow, no background or checkerboard, no text, no multiple poses, no UI. Genuine transparent alpha. Matches brown olive survivor and ruined gray streets.

## gate

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single ACTUAL closed industrial steel security gate sprite on transparent background for top-down 2D pixel game. Tall narrow rectangular rusty steel sliding shutter with heavy gray metal horizontal ribs, faded yellow-black hazard stripe border and a small central yellow radiation trefoil symbol without any text. Very slight overhead 2D RPG front-face view. Aspect ratio gate body around 1 width to 2.5 height. Full object centered with small transparent margin. Crisp chunky pixel art, olive charcoal and rust palette, dark edges, readable simple silhouette. Gate only, no wall outside its border, no people, no ground, no cast shadow, no frame, no grid, no lettering, no UI, genuine transparent alpha.

## panel

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single ACTUAL 2D Unity game control console sprite on transparent background. Small industrial upright yellow ochre metal control panel on short support, worn and rusty, cyan glowing rectangular screen at upper half, two simple small indicator buttons and a lever lower down. Front with slightly visible top as classic overhead 2D RPG game prop, not isometric. Sharp chunky pixel art about 32x48 logical pixels, dark outline. Full isolated console centered with small transparent margin. No writing, no letters, no person, no robot, no ground, no cast shadow, no background or checkerboard, no UI, no grid. Genuine transparent alpha.
